01 — Color

Ten values, used in proportion

Roughly 70% paper and bone, 25% ink, 5% accent. More accent than that and the system stops reading as institutional.

Stone#7A766AHairlines, borders — not body text
Stone dark#5E5B52Secondary text, eyebrows
Bone#EAE7DFAlternating section ground
Bone warm#E0DCD1Rules; text on dark bands

Accent#8C5A3CLinks, numerals, one rule per section

  • Ink on paper8.9 : 1  AAA
  • Stone dark on paper6.5 : 1  AA
  • Accent on paper5.5 : 1  AA
  • Bone on ink7.5 : 1  AAA
  • Stone on paper4.4 : 1  large text and rules only
  • Rule dark on ink3.2 : 1  non-text, AA

03 — Mark

A frame on a plinth

A capital P inside a thin frame, over a baseline rule that runs past it. Inline SVG in currentColor, so it takes the color of whatever sits around it.

Do

  • Keep clear space equal to the mark's own height on all four sides.
  • Let it inherit color — accent on paper, bone on ink.
  • Pair it with the wordmark at 0.7rem separation.

Do not

  • Set it below 24px; the interior of the P closes up.
  • Add gradients, shadows, rotation, or a second color.
  • Stretch the frame to fit a container.

04 — Layout

Whitespace is the design

Container is 72rem at most. Bands alternate paper, bone, ink, paper — never two dark bands in a row. Three columns above 768px, one below, and no breakpoint in between.

Rules of the grid

  • Band padding: clamp(4rem, 9vw, 7.5rem)
  • Gutter: clamp(1.25rem, 4vw, 3rem)
  • Hairlines 1px; one 2px accent rule per section at most
  • Measure: 62ch body, 34ch standfirst, 20–28ch display

When a section looks weak, add space above the heading — not color inside it.

05 — Voice

Short declaratives, named standards

Sounds right

  • “We make art easy.”
  • “Relationships matter. Communities matter. Art builds both.”
  • “More than forty years of experience.”
  • Plain verbs: plan, build, manage, steward, navigate, maintain.

Sounds wrong

  • “Solutions,” “passionate,” “world-class,” “leverage.”
  • Exclamation points.
  • Any claim not attached to a standard, a year count, or a named client type.
  • Rewriting the descriptor line. It is fixed.

The ellipsis in “Our Services …” and “… to make art easy” is theirs. It stays. Full written guide: BRAND.md.
