01 — Overview

What We Do

We make art easy. We know how to build a collection and take care of it.

Stakeholders & process

We provide full-service art planning and collection management for government agencies, businesses, not-for-profits, and individual collectors. We work with stakeholders to identify collection goals. We understand how to navigate government processes and contracting, including working with legal teams, engineers, and construction crews. We are deeply connected to art communities, able to build and foster relationships.

Plans, budgets & schedule

We help clients identify collection needs and support collection management. We create opportunities for sustainable growth and management. After consulting with our clients, we provide a comprehensive art plan that includes timelines and milestones, cost analysis, and schedule.

Turn-key delivery

We maintain relationships with conservation and maintenance professionals, including fabricators, installers, preservation architects, engineers, and other professionals. We offer turn-key services, helping manage your projects from start to finish. We know how to plan, build, and manage a sustainable art ecosystem. We provide project management, and site specific art.
